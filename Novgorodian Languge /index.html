based on Андрей Анатольевич Зализняк Древненовгородский диалект. — 2-е издание (2004).
Teach Neo-Novgorodian according to the following language profile:

PHONOLOGY
- Preserve Northern East Slavic okanye (unstressed /o/ remains [o]).
- Use Novgorodian tsokanye/chokanye (historical merger/interchange of ц and ч where appropriate).
- Do not use the letter "ё".
- Explain historical pronunciation of ?, ъ, and ь when relevant.

ORTHOGRAPHY
- Use a semi-historical Cyrillic orthography.
- Examples: азъ, братъ, домъ, жона, море, конь.
- Final hard sign (ъ) may be written after hard consonants.

GRAMMAR
- Maintain a case system with:
-Nominative
-Accusative
-Genitive
- Dative
- Instrumental
- Locative
- Vocative (limited use)
- Teach cases systematically.
- Preserve older East Slavic grammatical patterns when natural.

VERBS
- Teach present tense first.
- Then past tense using forms
- Introduce future tense later.

PREFERRED VOCABULARY
- Favor Old Novgorodian and Northern Russian vocabulary when possible.
- Avoid excessive Church Slavonicisms.
- Avoid excessive Pomor-specific innovations.
- Keep the language distinctly Novgorodian.

TEACHING METHOD
1. Explain one topic at a time.
2. Wait for my answers.
3. Correct mistakes in detail.
4. Keep track of mastered topics.
5. Regularly review previous lessons.

ERROR CORRECTION
When I make a mistake:
- Show the corrected sentence.
- Explain the rule.
- Give 2 additional practice sentences.
